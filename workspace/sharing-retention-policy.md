# Sharing and retention policy -- the practice business (2026-11-18)

One page an owner can read. The practice business is invented: no real name, address, phone or password goes in the ask or on this page.

**AI-assisted, directed by me**

## 1. Where files live

Business files such as invoices, customer lists and price lists are stored in the `Shop files` shared drive. Working drafts that are not yet business records may remain in the employee's My Drive until they are reviewed and moved.

## 2. Sharing outside the business

External sharing is restricted using the settings available in the Admin console. Customer information must not be publicly accessible. The `customer-list` file must have General access set to Restricted. External sharing is only permitted when approved by the business owner.

## 3. Who can sign in

Only authorised staff may sign in using their own work accounts. Users must enrol in 2-Step Verification. Devices must use basic device management and a screen lock where these controls are available in the practice OU.

## 4. What we keep, how long, where

The business keeps invoices, customer records, price lists, rota records and important business correspondence. Records are retained for the period required by applicable legal and accounting requirements. The exact retention period must be confirmed with the accountant before records are deleted. Business records are kept in the `Shop files` shared drive.

## 5. The monthly export

The business owner should arrange a monthly export of important business data. The export is stored in a separate drive controlled by the owner, not in the public project repository. The owner should open a file from the export to confirm that the backup is usable.

## 6. Who holds admin

The business owner retains responsibility for administrator access. A second authorised administrator or manager should also be available so that the business does not depend on one person's account. Administrator access is limited to people who need it for their duties.

## 7. When someone leaves

The owner confirms the employee's last working day and decides how their email will be handled. The employee's account is suspended, not immediately deleted. Password access and active sessions are reset, recovery methods and connected applications are reviewed, and business files, calendars, groups and shared-drive responsibilities are transferred to an authorised manager. Data is retained for the required period before any approved deletion.
